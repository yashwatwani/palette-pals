Our story

The Pals Behind Palette Pals

Art brought us together long before Palette Pals existed. Always by hand, always with heart.

How it began

What started as a shared dream

What started as a shared dedication to creating gradually became a shared dream. We found ourselves chasing the same goals, encouraging each other's growth, and discovering that our passion for art was stronger together than apart. As our friendship grew, so did our vision, and that's how Palette Pals was born.

Today, we're a creative duo creating murals, live wedding paintings, doodle booths, workshops, illustrations, and custom artworks. Every project is approached with the same curiosity, collaboration, and care that brought us together in the first place.

Because for us, great art isn't just about what we create; it's about the people, stories, and experiences we create it with.

Meet the pals

Two artists with one shared vision

Palette Pals is more than a name; it's a reflection of how we work. We believe the best ideas come from collaboration, curiosity, and a willingness to keep learning. Every day, we challenge each other to think differently, create thoughtfully, and bring authenticity to everything we do.
