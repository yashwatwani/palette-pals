What we do

Creative Experiences, Crafted for Every Occasion

Every piece is custom, hand-painted and made just for you. Here's where we shine.

Live Wedding Painting

We set up an easel at your celebration and paint the day in real time, so your guests watch a canvas come alive.

2D & 3D Murals

Statement walls for homes, cafés, clinics and offices, from flat painted scenes to sculpted, textured 3D art.

Doodle Booth

A live doodle wall for events and brands, where we fill a space with playful hand-drawn art as guests watch.

Workshops & Classes

Hands-on painting sessions for groups, teams and beginners, guided by two artists who love to teach.
