About

Value meets duo.

Palette Pals is the creative brainchild of Hitanshi Watwani and Ashmeet Bharaj. Built on a shared passion for artistic expression and visual storytelling, our studio is dedicated to transforming spaces, inspiring minds, and building a vibrant creative community.

Our journey & expertise

Our artistic foundation began at the Government Institute of Fine Arts in Gwalior, where we both earned our Bachelor of Fine Arts. Driven by a desire to master the classic arts, we advanced our training in Hyderabad, at the Samsara Academy of Arts, immersing ourselves in the timeless classical oil painting techniques of the Old Masters.

That blend of formal academic training and traditional technique is what lets us bring depth, craftsmanship and soul to every project we take on.

We work as a pair on everything, from a single carved door panel to a wall that takes a fortnight. One of us is always holding the brush while the other is ten feet back, looking at the whole wall. Murals go wrong at the scale you cannot see from arm's length.

Working with us

What you can expect.

You get a scale drawing on a photo of your own wall before anything is painted. You get a fixed price, not an hourly one. We bring our own materials, protect the room, and clean up properly.

We use premium materials throughout: Asian Paints for walls, Winsor & Newton oils for canvas work.

We will tell you if we think the wall you have chosen is the wrong one, or if the budget does not match the idea. That conversation is free and it saves everybody a month.
