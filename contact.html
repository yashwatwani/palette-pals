Before you write

Questions we get asked a lot.

What does it cost?

It depends on size, detail and surface. We quote a fixed price after seeing a photo of the wall, never by the hour.

How long does it take?

Most walls are two to six days on site. Relief and carved work takes longer. Live wedding canvases are finished on the day.

Do you travel?

Yes. We are based in Delhi and have worked in Gwalior, Rajasthan and Maharashtra. Travel is quoted separately.

Will it last?

We seal every finished wall so it can be wiped clean, and we leave you touch-up paint in the exact colours.
