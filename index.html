The pals

Two artists, one wall at a time.

Hitanshi and Ashmeet work as a pair on everything. Two people means one holds the line while the other steps back and checks it, and it means a wall that would take a month takes a fortnight.

We paint flat and we paint raised. Some walls want colour, some want a texture you notice only when the afternoon light crosses it. Knowing which is which is most of the job.

More about us →

How it goes

From a bare wall to a finished one.

Talk

You tell us the room, the light, the budget and what the space is for. We tell you honestly whether a mural is the right answer.

Sketch

You get a drawing to scale, on a photo of your actual wall, with the palette. Nothing gets painted until you are happy.

Paint

We arrive with everything, protect the room and work. Most walls take two to six days. You can watch or you can leave us to it.

Seal

We finish, seal it against cleaning and scuffs, clear up properly and leave you the touch-up paint.
